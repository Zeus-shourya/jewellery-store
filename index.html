About the house

Jewellery is bought on trust

Gold and silver are more than ornaments. They mark weddings, births, festivals and first salaries. That is why we treat every sale as the start of a long relationship and not a single transaction.

At Swarn Rajat you will find hallmarked gold, sterling silver, and people who will tell you plainly what a piece weighs, what it is made of and what it costs.

Our promise

Four things you can count on

01

Certified purity

Gold carries its BIS hallmark and silver is stamped 925, so the purity on the bill is the purity in your hand.

02

Open pricing

Every piece is weighed in front of you. The bill lists weight, the day's rate, making charges and GST on separate lines.

03

Made to order

Bring a photograph, a sketch or an heirloom to rework. We design and make the piece around you.

04

Fair exchange

Old gold is tested and valued in front of you when you exchange it or upgrade to something new.
