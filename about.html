Our story

Jewellery is bought on trust

Most people buy gold only a few times in their lives: for a wedding, for a daughter, for a festival, for the day they can finally afford it. Each of those days matters, and so does the person standing behind the counter.

Swarn Rajat Jewellers was built on a simple idea. Tell the customer exactly what they are buying. Show the hallmark. Put the piece on the scale where they can see it. Write the weight, the rate and the making charge on the bill, line by line.

We keep gold for the occasions that call for it and silver for everything in between, so there is something here for a bride, a newborn, a brother, and for you.

Purity

What it says is what it is

Gold is sold with its hallmark and silver with its 925 stamp. If the bill says 22 karat, the metal is 22 karat.

Craft

Made by hand, checked by eye

Every clasp is tested, every stone is checked in its setting and every surface is polished before a piece reaches the counter.

Honesty

Nothing hidden in the price

Weight, the day's rate, making charges and tax are shown separately, so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

From metal to ornament

How a piece is made

Whether it comes from our showcase or from your own sketch, every piece passes through the same five stages.

01

Design

We start with a drawing, agreed with you, and an estimate of weight.

02

Forming

The metal is alloyed to the right karat, then cast or worked by hand into shape.

03

Setting

Stones, kundan and enamel work are set one by one and secured.

04

Polish

Filing, finishing and polishing bring out the colour and the shine.

05

Hallmark

The piece is tested for purity, stamped, weighed and billed.

Purity guide

What the numbers mean

Pure gold and pure silver are too soft to wear every day, so they are blended with a little of another metal for strength. The stamp tells you how much is pure.

GradeStampPure metalBest for
24K gold99999.9% goldCoins and bars. Too soft for everyday jewellery.
22K gold91691.6% goldTraditional jewellery: bridal sets, bangles, chains and necklaces.
18K gold75075% goldDiamond and stone-set pieces, where the extra strength holds the setting.
Fine silver99999.9% silverCoins, bars and pooja articles.
Sterling silver92592.5% silverJewellery: bracelets, anklets, rings, chains and earrings.
